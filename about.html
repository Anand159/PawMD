About Us

Because Your Pet’s Health Deserves the Best of Technology

At PawMd, we believe that pets aren’t just animals—they’re family. And just like any family member, they deserve care that’s smart, proactive, and always within reach. That’s why we created a platform that brings together advanced health monitoring, AI-driven insights, and 24/7 veterinary support—all in one seamless experience.

Vision

To become the world's most trusted digital ecosystem for pet health—connecting pets, owners, and vets through data, empathy, and innovation.

Mission

To empower pet parents and veterinary professionals with intelligent tools that make pet health monitoring effortless, efficient, and accessible anytime, anywhere.

What We Do?

PawMd combines cutting-edge wearable technology, powerful AI algorithms, and a secure telemedicine platform to deliver end-to-end healthcare for your pets. From early detection of illnesses to virtual vet consultations, we’re building a smarter way to care.

Who We Serve?

Pet Lovers

Those who are seeking peace of mind and better wellness for their companions

Veterinarians

Those who want to extend care beyond the clinic

Support Staff

Who are needing streamlined appointment and communication tools

Why Choose Us

Catch Health Issues Early

Smart collars & AI constantly monitor and analyze your pet’s vitals and behavior to detect early warning signs.

Instant Vet Access, 24/7

Book virtual consultations, get digital prescriptions, and chat with certified vets—without stepping outside.

All Records. One Dashboard.

From medical history to medication tracking and growth charts, PawPulse keeps everything organized and accessible.

Peace of Mind for Every Pet Parent

Real-time alerts, GPS tracking, and automated care reminders keep you in the know—even when you’re away.

Built With Love, Driven By Data

Our team of veterinarians, developers, data scientists, and pet lovers are working together to redefine pet wellness. We’re not just building software—we’re building a healthier future for the animals who bring joy to our lives.
